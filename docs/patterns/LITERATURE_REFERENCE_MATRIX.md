## Signal Category Justification

The signal families are grounded in:
- Attachment theory (Bowlby, Ainsworth)
- Boundary theory (Leary, Goffman)
- Self-determination theory (Deci, Ryan)
- Health communication research (Cline, Dutta-Bergman)

**Important**: These references support CONTEXTUAL SIGNAL detection, NOT pathology diagnosis. The system maps interaction patterns to contextual categories for adaptive response purposes only.

## Block Conditions

All literature references support contextual signals, NOT clinical diagnoses:
- No references to diagnostic criteria (DSM, ICD)
- No pathology-based signal categories
- Focus on attachment, boundaries, self-regulation, and self-care seeking

## Notes

- References are provided for academic grounding
- Signal families do not constitute clinical labels
- The system does not claim to detect or diagnose conditions
- All signals are for researcher review and system adaptation purposes only
